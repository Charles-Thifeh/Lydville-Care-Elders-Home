Who We Are

A home built on dignity, love & community

Lydville Care and Elders' Home is a premier senior care and home facility designed to provide a safe, comfortable, and nurturing environment for seniors. Located at Isheri Brooks Estate, Kara Isheri (near Ojodu Berger), Ogun State, our care centre combines compassionate care with modern amenities to ensure that every resident lives with dignity, fulfilment, and joy.

Whether your loved one requires assisted living or independent living, we are dedicated to meeting their unique needs with professionalism and love. We understand that ageing comes with unique challenges, and our mission is to provide a supportive space where seniors can thrive physically, emotionally, and socially.

Modern & Secure Campus
Independent residences, assisted living spaces, communal areas, and a relaxation & leisure centre.
Holistic Approach
Addressing physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing — including healthy feeding and supervised medication.
Personalised Plans
Every resident's care is tailored specifically to their needs and personal preferences.
Vibrant Community
A place where lifelong friendships are formed and meaningful connections are made every day.
Our Vision

Quality care for the elderly in a loving and nourishing home.

We envision a Nigeria where every senior citizen can access world-class care within a warm, home-like environment that respects their history, honours their individuality, and enriches their golden years.

Our Mission

Personalised care that nourishes the whole person.

To deliver personalised care and a loving elders' home that meets the physical, medical, emotional, spiritual, and social needs of each resident — while fostering a sense of healthy living, purpose, belonging, and happiness within the home.

Products & Services

Care tailored to every need

From full assisted living to flexible short stays and in-home care giver support, we have a solution for every senior and every family.

Assisted Living

Lydville Assisted Living Facility

Personalised care plans for seniors requiring help with daily activities. Ideal for seniors who are not able to live at home safely but don't require intensive medical care.

  • Help with dressing, bathing & grooming
  • Transportation & housekeeping support
  • Cooking & meal preparation
  • Medication management & monitoring
Independent Living

Lydville Independent Living Community

Comfortable and private residences for seniors who want to maintain their independence with access to support services when needed. Loneliness is one of the biggest challenges the elderly face — independent living allows seniors to socialise and live among like individuals.

  • Private, comfortable residences
  • Active social community & events
  • Optional support services on demand
  • Safe, secure, and professionally staffed
Short Stay / Respite

Lydville Short Stay Living Facility

A flexible short-stay option for seniors who desire a change of environment or wish to experience our community. Also ideal for families who don't want to leave their senior loved ones at home alone while on holiday or out of town.

  • Temporary & respite placements
  • Full access to all community facilities
  • Flexible stay durations
  • Peace of mind for travelling families
Professional Care Givers

Lydville Care Givers

Our professional care givers ensure the wellbeing and safety of those in their care, acting as a liaison between the resident, their family, and healthcare providers.

  • Assistance with bathing, dressing & meals
  • Health monitoring & medication administration
  • Coordination of medical appointments
  • Compassionate daily companionship
