Our Campus

Daily Life

A day in the life at Lydville Care

Every day at Lydville is filled with purpose, connection, and comfort. Here's a glimpse of what life looks like for our residents.

Morning

A refreshing start to the day

Start the day with gentle exercise or a morning walk in our serene outdoor environment — breathing in fresh air and setting a positive tone for the hours ahead.
Enjoy a hearty, nutritious breakfast lovingly prepared by our dedicated chefs, tailored to individual dietary requirements and served in our warm communal dining area.
Afternoon

Engagement, activity & community

Participate in group activities such as public affairs discussions, painting, indoor games, book clubs, or music sessions — keeping the mind active and connections alive.
Relish a delicious, chef-prepared lunch in the warm company of fellow residents — a highlight of the day filled with conversation, laughter, and good food.
Evening

Winding down in warmth & comfort

Relax with a quiet movie night, a cultural event, or a storytelling session — evenings at Lydville are filled with the warmth of community and the comfort of familiar faces.
Unwind in the privacy of your cosy, fully furnished residence after a light dinner — ending each day with a deep sense of belonging, safety, and peace.
Health & Wellness

Comprehensive support, every hour

Our health and wellness programmes go beyond physical care — we nurture the whole person.

Health & Wellness Programmes

Regular health checks, tailored exercise routines, and wellness initiatives designed to promote physical and mental wellbeing for every resident.

  • Routine health monitoring & checks
  • Supervised medication management
  • Coordinated medical appointments
  • Physical exercise & mobility routines

Recreational & Social Activities

A rich variety of social, educational, and recreational programmes to keep every resident engaged, stimulated, and actively connected to their community.

  • Group discussions & educational sessions
  • Arts, crafts, music & cultural events
  • Indoor games & movie nights
  • Storytelling sessions & book clubs

24/7 Professional Security

Round-the-clock professional care and on-site security to ensure every resident is safe, looked after, and at ease — no matter the time of day or night.

  • On-site security personnel at all times
  • Emergency response systems in all rooms
  • Secure, gated campus access
  • Night-time care staff always present

Relaxation & Leisure Centre

A dedicated space for rest, recreation, and personal wellbeing — offering residents a calm retreat to unwind, recharge, and enjoy quiet moments of restorative peace.

  • Quiet relaxation & reading spaces
